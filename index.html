// how it works

Three steps. No jargon.

  1. We talk

    A free 30-minute chat, in person in Kingston or by phone. You tell me what your customers need to find.

  2. I build it

    You send your logo, photos, and the basics. I write the rest with you and show you a draft before anything goes live.

  3. I look after it

    Your site goes live. On the shield plan or pay as you go, when something changes in your business, I change it on your site.
